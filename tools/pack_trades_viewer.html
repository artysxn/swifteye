Prices at the buy and at the sale

Everything is for the side bought, in cents, the way the bot's chart shows it. Bookmakers: 1 ÷ odds (the bot's own measure, margin not removed); for a bet against a player or team, 100 − that, with the odds on them in brackets. Polymarket: what the bought token could be sold at (bid) and bought at (ask). ★ = the bookmaker whose move triggered the buy.
